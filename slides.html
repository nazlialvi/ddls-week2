One caveat: stain is not biology

Giemsa staining strength and image quality vary between slides.

Heavy stain can expose purple marks or create clumps; pale or unevenly lit crops can hide faint rings. The model may learn contrast and stain mix rather than parasite morphology.

So 90.91% describes this held-out slide mix, not general clinical accuracy.
